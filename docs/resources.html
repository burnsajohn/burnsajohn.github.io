Tools

Predict Trophic Mode

A computational tool to predict eukaryote function from genome or transcriptome data. Given a set of predicted proteins, it estimates whether an organism is phototrophic, phagotrophic, or both, without anyone having to watch the cell feed.

Deep-sea genomes and transcriptomes

Sequence data from the in situ digital synthesis work: a salp genome, plus reference transcriptome assemblies and annotations for siphonophores, ctenophores, larvaceans, jellyfish, tunicates, and a pelagic polychaete, several of them preserved in situ before recovery.

  • Genome of Pegea sp., GenBank accession JAQGEQ000000000
  • Reads and transcriptomes, NCBI BioProject PRJNA919492
  • Assemblies and annotations on Zenodo: Pegea · Tomopteris · Marrus claudanielis · Erenna

Per-specimen accessions, imaging data, and library details are tabulated in the data paper.

Method paper

Burns Lab, Bigelow Laboratory for Ocean Sciences, East Boothbay, Maine
